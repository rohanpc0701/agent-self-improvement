04the discipline that mattered

The original gate would have printed “STRUCTURE confirmed.” A blind tightening refused it.

The shipped decision rule accepted any denominator whose CI excluded zero. Under that rule this run passes the gate, reports recovery 0.695, and prints “STRUCTURE → Q2 platform check”.

A 13-agent adversarial audit found the rule was also sign-blind — a teacher effect measured as significantly harmful would have divided by a negative denominator and printed “KNOWLEDGE load-bearing.” The fix — one-sided gate plus a magnitude floor — was written and committed while nobody had seen a single per-arm mean.

the eleventh caught failure mode — and the first flattering one

Every previous bug this program caught made results look worse than truth. This one would have made them look better. Blind pre-registration is the only reason the difference is visible rather than buried.

07the ask

Q2-SELF-PLAN: resolve the mechanism directly

~$35 and one week. One experiment, not a program.
design
Drop arm C. Its job is done. Test B − A directly instead of dividing by a weak denominator.
n
80 — the 40 already run plus the remaining 40 of the frozen validation split. k=3, 3 judge passes, paired.
plus
A bare arm at n=20: no preamble at all. Q1’s design is biased toward STOP because the restatement control may itself help — this bounds that bias. Secondary measurement, changes no branch.
power
CI half-width on B − A was ~4.0 at n=40; expect ~2.8 at n=80, which resolves a +3.6 effect if it is real [design — assumes Q1 variance holds]
branch rule committed before the first generation call — CI excludes zero → self-plan works at 27B, next is held-out confirmation. CI includes zero at n=80 → structure-alone is dead at this scale, and teacher-priced plans become the product.
08scope

What is deliberately not being asked for

  • Cached teacher plans. That was the KNOWLEDGE branch’s action, and KNOWLEDGE did not fire. It re-enters only on a Q2 null.
  • Any reopening of frozen memory. Dead four ways across three domains.
  • Fine-tuning. Still outside scope; nothing here forces that conversation yet.
  • A cross-stack claim. Every number on these slides is scoped to OpenRouter with the student provider-pinned. Platform replication precedes any company-wide document.

Shipping in parallel, with no research risk: a cascade — student by default, teacher escalation on low-confidence items, confidence calibrated on already-graded cells. That is product architecture, and will be presented as architecture, not as learning.
